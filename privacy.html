Office Run: Privacy

The game itself collects nothing. No account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own. We never see who you are or how you play.

What stays on your phone: your best distances, coffee, outfits, charms, the race book, your settings, and a count of runs used to space out ad breaks. They are saved on the device and never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads: the game is free and shows ads between some runs, never during one, and never in a race. You can also choose to watch an ad on the end-of-run screen to keep that run going. The ads are supplied by Google AdMob. To choose and deliver an ad, count it and stop fraud, Google's software in the app receives: your phone's internet address (from which it can tell roughly what country or city you are in), an identifier for the device or the app, which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and technical reports such as crashes and loading times. This goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Personalised ads: iOS asks whether Office Run may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google may use your phone's advertising identifier to choose ads based on what it knows about you. If you do not, you see the same number of ads, chosen without that. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

No Ads: one purchase removes the ads for good. Apple takes the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple ID, only whether the purchase exists, which the app asks the App Store on your phone. Once it is bought, Google's ad software is not started and receives nothing.

Your choices in Europe: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your choice before the first ad, and you can change it at any time with "Privacy choices" on the Privacy page inside the game.

Racing nearby: when you open a race, the game talks only to other phones on the same Wi-Fi or nearby, to share runner positions during that race. Nothing from a race goes to the internet or to us. iOS asks your permission first. There are no ads in a race.

Children: the game is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information itself from anyone, of any age, and its ads are limited to those rated suitable for a general audience.
