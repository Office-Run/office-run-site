Office Run: Privacy

Office Run collects nothing. No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

What stays on your phone: your best distances, coffee, outfits, charms, the race book and your settings. They are saved on the device and never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Racing nearby: when you open a race, the game talks only to other phones on the same Wi-Fi or nearby, to share runner positions during that race. Nothing goes to the internet or to us. iOS asks your permission first.

Children: the game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes: if this ever changes, this page and the App Store listing will say so first.
