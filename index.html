Office Run

Run out of the job, through the way home, and do it all again tomorrow.

Help

Controls: swipe left or right to change lane or take a corner, up to jump, down to slide.

Progress: your coffee, outfits, charms and best distances are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Racing nearby: both phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi and to allow Office Run to find devices on the local network (Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network).

A purchase on a new phone: open the shop and tap Restore purchase.

Contact

Found a bug or stuck somewhere? Write to Support-Office-Run@proton.me and say which phone you have and what was on the screen.

Privacy

The game itself collects nothing about you; the free ads come from Google AdMob. The full policy: privacy.html.

Office Run is a game for iPhone and Android.
